# Origin, Creator and Technical Thesis

## Creator

**LeeWay Formula was created and developed by Leonard Lee**, founder of **LeeWay Industries**.

The Formula is part of the broader LeeWay engineering thesis:

> **AI should increase human capability, not replace human responsibility.**

This repository records the mathematics, engineering implementations, tests, failures, repairs, receipts and reproducibility evidence needed to challenge that thesis scientifically.

## Why the Formula was created

The motivating engineering problem is fragmentation. Modern AI/computing systems often make decisions in one place while memory, hardware telemetry, storage, tools, verification and learning live elsewhere. This creates unnecessary compute, context, data movement, storage, memory pressure, latency, retries, opaque model discretion and human oversight cost.

LeeWay's proposed answer is to represent the environment mathematically, minimize unnecessary physical/cognitive work, route execution through governed capabilities, and require evidence before learning.

## What is new vs what is inherited

LeeWay does **not** claim ownership of binary arithmetic, Shannon information theory, queueing theory, Kalman filtering, Boolean algebra, matrix multiplication, FastCDC, Opus, HEVC, AV1, PCIe, USB, or other established mathematics/standards.

LeeWay's contribution is the **composition and governance architecture** around bounded state representation, the historical 16×6 pattern kernel, Formula families, hardware/compute adapters, separation of decision/execution/proof/learning, receipt-based evidence, the Formula Funnel, and reconstructable state/representation research.

## Independence from models and hardware

The Formula is mathematically defined independently of any specific LLM, CPU, GPU, operating system or agent.

That means the decision rule does not originate from an LLM and the state law does not change because the GPU vendor changes. It does **not** mean equations physically execute without a substrate. A machine must still evaluate the arithmetic and carry out the selected actuation.

## Scientific posture

LeeWay makes bold claims only at the level supported by evidence.

- 11.18× GPU VRAM working-set reduction is not called 11.18× total model compression.
- 3.26 GiB Android cache reclaim is not called media reconstruction.
- Exact SHA reconstruction is separated from behavioral equivalence.
- 16×/32×/64× remain targets until the exact denominator and acceptance test pass.
